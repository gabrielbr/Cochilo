# Audit: the night as one thing (0.42.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable critique, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13)

## Scope

The owner: Cochilo didn't separate naps from the night, and a night with wakings wasn't one night.

What changed:
- `Event.night` and `upForDay` (Room 3 → 4, backup version 4);
- `domain/Nights.kt` and `NightsTest`;
- `Entries.startSleep` (it guesses), `upForDay`, `setNight`;
- `Stats.nightWakings`;
- Today's sleep card: the label, the swap link, and the night-waking state;
- the notification's two buttons and the widget's Back to sleep;
- Napper's nights.

Screens looked at:
- `today_night_waking_ptBR_light` (new), `today_sleeping_dark`, `today_night_light_sleeping`,
  `today_sleeping_font200_light`, `today_sleeping_in_the_sling_light`,
  `today_sleeping_fabric_tropical_dark`.

## What failed first, and the fix

| Found | Fix |
|---|---|
| The swap link sat between the label and the timer, splitting the card's hierarchy | Moved under "Since …", after the number |
| During a night waking Today showed the next-nap card ("Past the window" at 3am) | Hidden while a night is open |
| A night waking asked "where did she sleep?" in the dark | Asked once, at Up for the day, for the night's first sleep |
| A forgotten Up for the day would keep the night open | A night closes after 4 hours without sleep |
