# Audit: less is more (0.43.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (less-is-more skill; impeccable critique, native;
graphic-design-fundamentals §13)

## Scope

The owner: "reduce clutter and less valuable information and actions. Think of less is more."

The research is in the new skill, [`.claude/skills/less-is-more/SKILL.md`](../../.claude/skills/less-is-more/SKILL.md):
- calm technology (Weiser and Brown 1995; Amber Case 2015);
- progressive disclosure;
- Hick–Hyman, with its limits;
- reducing load without stripping features;
- what baby-tracker makers have learned.

Every element on Today, Day and Patterns went through its five questions:
1. Helps now
2. Duplicate
3. Understand once
4. Weak for the goal
5. Rare

Screens looked at:
- `today_light`, `today_ptBR_light`, `today_dark`, `today_font200_light`, `today_tablet_light`;
- `today_sleeping_dark`, `today_night_waking_ptBR_light`;
- `day_light`, `day_hour_by_hour_light`, `day_dragging_a_sleep_light`;
- `patterns_light`, `patterns_font200_light`, `patterns_ptBR_dark`, `patterns_changes_light`.

## What was cut, moved or merged

| Element | Answer | Now |
|---|---|---|
| "Today" title on Today | 2: the tab and the header name it | Gone; the sleep card leads |
| Header "Awake · 40m" on Today | 2: the card says the same number, bigger | Hidden on Today; kept on Day and Patterns, where it's the only place |
| Sleep card "i" | 3: explains the card once | Gone; the entry sheets keep theirs |
| The prediction's numbers (two meta lines) | 3: read to trust it, not every time | "How it's guessed" (one tap) |
| Day ring caption | 2: the ring and its legend say it | Gone |
| "Hold a sleep and drag" hint | 3: a gesture hint | Until the first drag (`Prefs.adjustHintSeen`) |
| Daylight nudge during a night waking | 1 fails at night | Never shown while a night is open |
| Patterns › Questions about sleep | 2: the header's chat icon, on every tab | Gone |
| Patterns week rows: total sleep first | 4: the night is the goal | The night first: night sleep, longest stretch, night wakings, usual bedtime (new), then the total and the day |
| Feeds, Diapers (two rows) | 4 | One row: "Feeds · diapers" |
| Rhythm meta (two sentences) | 3 | One short sentence |

Kept on purpose:
- Earlier chips: the common entry.
- The swap link: one-tap correction of the guess.
- Running: what's on now.
- The daylight nudge by day: helps now.
- The changes list and the places: what changed.

Findings:
- **P3:** the 48dp "How it's guessed" target leaves 16dp of air under the link. It stays, because
  the target size rule wins.

## Graphic design fundamentals (§13)

- **Hierarchy:** pass. Today opens on the number and the one mint action.
- **Contrast:** pass. No new colours.
- **Alignment and grid:** pass. Removing the title leaves the ScrollPage's 32dp top.
- **Spacing:** pass. Spacing comes from tokens only (`space6` where the caption was).
- **Typography:** pass. One meta line per card.
- **Colour:** pass. Teal is still the one action.
- **Consistency:** pass.
- **Simplicity:** pass. 5 elements fewer on Today awake (title, status, "i", two meta lines); on Patterns 3 fewer and 1 added (bedtime).
- **Real-size test:** pass at 200% font (`today_font200_light`, `patterns_font200_light`).
- **Accessibility:** pass.
- **Production:** pass. EN and PT strings are in; unused strings were removed.
