# 0.58.0: the floating day ring and routine music (four-skill audit)

The owner asked for two things:
- "Update the circled timeline to something more user-friendly … the elements are too small and
  very strict to the basic forms, so I want stuff to kind of float around and it's more clear and
  less crowded."
- A place in Settings for Spotify or YouTube Music links for the routine, with an embedded player
  "if possible", researched first.

**How each skill was used.** All four were loaded with the Skill tool in this session (this
conversation): `ux-user-flows`, `less-is-more`, `impeccable` (Cochilo's copy; its context launcher
ran and found no PRODUCT.md, so these scoped changes used the code as context, with
`audit.native.md`, `android.md`, `craft-floor.md` and `critique.md` read) and
`graphic-design-fundamentals`. Their checklists were applied to the new screenshots below. There
is no emulator here, so the screens were judged from Paparazzi at real size, in light, dark,
pt-BR and 200% font:
- the ring: `day_today_expected_light`, `day_filter_all_light`, `day_ptBR_dark`, `day_font200_light`;
- the music: `routine_music_light`, `routine_music_wrong_ptBR_dark`, `routine_with_music_light`,
  `settings_light`.

The owner's screenshot came from another app. Its mechanics were taken (bigger marks that float,
times along the circle, stacking); its look was not. The ring keeps Cochilo's own tokens, icons,
fabrics and colours.

## 1. ux-user-flows

**Job stories.**
- When I glance at yesterday at 3am, I want to see how the sleeps and feeds fell without
  squinting, so I can tell whether the day was calm.
- When it's time for bed, I want our lullaby playlist with one tap, so I can start the routine with
  the baby in my arms.

**The embed decision** (research in [rituals.md](../rituals.md)):
- **Spotify:** its embed plays only 30-second previews in an Android app.
- **YouTube Music:** no embed exists.
- **Any embed:** it stops with the screen off.
- **The play build:** it has no internet at all.

An embedded player would fail the job, so the link opens in the music app instead. That is
Jakob's law (people already know their music app) and Tesler's law (the music app absorbs
playback, queue and screen-off).

the legend (Nielsen #6, recognition over recall). Each mark is now its own icon.

## 2. impeccable

Native Android, Operate mode. Critique (Assessment A): the ring now has a clear figure-ground. The
sleep band is the first read, the moons and times the second, the floating entries the third.
Clusters stack instead of piling up, which was the owner's "less crowded". Peak-end: a busy day
now ends in a readable picture, not a tangle.

Audit (native, against android.md):

| # | Dimension | Score | Key finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accessibility | 4 | One spoken summary for the ring; the middle and the labels grow to the axis cap (1.5×), so nothing overlaps at 200%; music buttons 52dp, fields labelled |
| 2 | Performance | 4 | Layout is pure and remembered per day; text measured once per label |
| 3 | Appearance & theming | 4 | Tokens only (`space6` track, `kind-badge`, `outline`, `surface`, meta); light, dark and night |
| 4 | Platform conformance | 4 | Links open through Android's `ACTION_VIEW`, to the music app first |
| 5 | Adaptivity | 4 | The ring sizes to the card (up to 320dp); foldable and tablet snapshots re-recorded |
| **Total** | | **20/20** | |

Findings:
- **P2, fixed:** at 200% the middle total wrapped onto the floating marks. It now stays on one
  line and grows only to the axis cap.
- **P3, fixed:** the hour notches read as stray dashes, so they were removed; the hour labels say it.
- **P3, fixed:** the side hour labels turned sideways and "06" read "90". The hours now stay
  upright; only the sleep times turn along the circle.

No P0 or P1.

## 4. less-is-more

| Element | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Inner ring of thin arcs (feeds, pumping, daylight) | 2, duplicate of the list | Removed: each entry is one floating icon; durations stay in the hour-by-hour list |
| Diaper dots | 2 | Removed: diapers float as icons like everything else |
| Hour notches | none | Removed |
| Four hour labels | 1, orientation | Kept, upright, and dropped where a sleep's time needs the spot |
| Sleep start and end times | 1 | New, along the outside; the ones that would overlap are dropped |
| A stack of 3+ entries | 2 | One icon plus "+N" |
| An embedded player | 4, fails the goal | Not built: the music app plays better |
| Music in the routine sheet | 1 | Shown only when links are saved |

| Dimension | 0–4 |
|---|---|

| Duplicates left | 4 |

## What was cut

The ring's inner arcs, its diaper dots and its hour notches. The embedded player was never built.
