# Audit: Ask Cochilo knows the log (0.38.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable critique, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13)

## Scope

The owner's screenshot: "o quão devem demorar as sonecas do Dante?" got "I can help with…".
They wrote: "Right now the AI is useless… we must connect our info to it… its entry point is
very hidden."

What changed:
- `NAP_LENGTH` (Assistant, and its answer strings in English and Portuguese).
- `Digest`, the model's facts.
- Grounding's number reading, plus the "couldn't check" and "still downloading" notes.
- The header's Ask icon (design system 0.27.0, patch 0024).

Screens looked at:
- `ask_nap_length_ptBR_light` (the owner's question, now answered) and `ask_not_sure_light`;
- the screens with the new header icon: `today_*`, `history_light`, `history_foldable_dark`,
  `history_font200_light`, `patterns_*` (34 images re-recorded).

## UX audit of Ask (what failed, and the fix)

| # | Found | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The entry was one row in Patterns, a screen for looking back, not for asking | High | The chat icon in the header of every tab, before Settings: 1 tap from anywhere |
| 2 | A common question ("how long should naps last") wasn't a topic | High | `NAP_LENGTH`, with her median, shortest and longest nap and the age's usual range |
| 3 | The model saw four lines, so it couldn't say anything about the baby | High | `Digest`: name, age, the 7 days one by one, today's sleeps, this week, the references |
| 4 | A reply with a usual number ("30 min to 2 h") was thrown away silently | High | Those numbers are now in the facts, and "05" reads as "5"; a reply that still fails is announced |
| 5 | While the model downloaded, a "not sure" reply gave no hint it was coming | Medium | "Still downloading. Ask again once it's ready" under the reply |
| 6 | Small invented numbers can slip through the check | Low, known | Documented; replies stay labelled "can make mistakes" |
