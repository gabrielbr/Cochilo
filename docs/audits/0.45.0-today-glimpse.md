# Audit: Today ends with Ask Cochilo; the filter goes to Day (0.45.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13;
less-is-more)

## Scope

The owner asked for four things:
- remove "Dia a dia" from Today;
- put its kind filter on the Day tab, above the timeline;
- make Today's card taller, with more of the fabric showing;
- add a card at the bottom with a glimpse from the AI, relevant to the baby's data.

What changed:
- **Today:** `HistoryDays` is removed. The new last card is `AskGlimpse`, and `glimpseTopic`
  (tested in `GlimpseTest`) picks its question.
- **Day:** the kind chips sit above "Hour by hour". One kind shows `EventList`, and "All" shows
  the timeline with drag to adjust.
- **Fabric frame:** the band is `space16` on top and `space4` around, with `space8` inside, top
  and bottom.
- **Ask:** the glimpse opens a new chat already holding that answer.

Screens looked at:
- `today_full_light`, `today_full_fabric_dark`, `today_light`, `today_font200_light`,
  `today_sleeping_dark`, `today_night_waking_ptBR_light`;
- `day_filter_feed_light`, `day_filter_all_light`.

## What failed first, and the fix

| Found | Fix |
|---|---|
| The glimpse first picked "What changes around 4 months?", whose only number is the baby's age | Stand-outs first, then an everyday question that has her numbers, one a day; never the age alone or the next sleep |
| A glimpse at 3am would be text to read at night | Hidden from 19:00 to 07:00 and during a night waking |
| With one kind picked, the drag hint and the timeline made no sense | One kind shows a plain list, as History did; the hint goes with the timeline |
