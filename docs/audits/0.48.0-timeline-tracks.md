# Audit: the timeline on two tracks (0.48.0)

Date: 2026-11-04 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13;
less-is-more)

## Scope

The owner's screenshot showed an 11-hour night: a tall bar with empty space beside it, then three
feeds below it with round badges and type almost as big as the night's. He asked for two tracks:
- a main track for sleep, naps and awake;
- a lighter one for everything else: outlined, in their colours, with clearly smaller words.

What changed:
- **Kit:** `TimelineStretch` (a whole stretch as one block: the main lane bar, its title, its
  entries beside it) and `TimelineEntry` (the secondary track) replace `TimelineRow` and
  `TimelineAwake`.
- **Day screen:** `VerticalTimeline` renders through them.

Screens looked at:
- `day_hour_by_hour_light`, `day_filter_all_light`, `day_dragging_a_sleep_light`,
  `day_today_expected_light`;
- the owner's dark-night case, by reading the code against his screenshot.

## What failed first, and the fix

| Found | Fix |
|---|---|
| A long sleep left a blank gap beside its bar, and its feeds sat below it | One block per stretch: the entries sit beside the bar they happened during |
| Feeds had the same round badge and nearly the same type size as sleep | No badge (the plain muted icon), bodySmall (14) and meta (13) against the label role's 16 bold |
| Awake showed a hollow ring as its icon and an outlined bar in the lane: the same thing twice | The ring is gone from the title; the outline says it |
| Feeds' marks were filled, competing with sleep | Outlines in the entry's colour (`outline` stroke); only sleep is filled |
