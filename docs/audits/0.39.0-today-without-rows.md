# Audit: Today without the log rows (0.39.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable critique, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13)

## Scope

The owner: "remove the register items of the home screen; they are not needed there anymore and
are confusing."

What changed:
- Removed `QuickLog` (the eight slide rows) and its section from Today.
- Removed its two screenshots (`quick_log_*`) and two strings that only it used.
- The empty state now points to the + .

Kept:
- `SlideRow`, still used by a running breastfeed's switch;
- `domain/QuickLog.kt` (what was done last), which the Log menu, the sheets and the widget use.

Screens looked at:
- `today_daylight_nudge_light`, `empty_today_light`;
- `today_sleeping_dark`, `today_night_light_sleeping`, `today_sleeping_in_the_sling_light`,
  `today_sleeping_fabric_tropical_dark`, `today_tablet_light`.

## Why it was confusing

- Two ways to log the same thing on one screen, and they looked different: rows on Today, a
  menu everywhere.
- The rows pushed the timeline and today's entries below the fold.
- The Log button already marks what was used last, so nothing that is remembered is lost.
