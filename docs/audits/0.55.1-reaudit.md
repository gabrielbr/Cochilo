# Re-audit: 0.51.0 to 0.55.0, with the four skills loaded (0.55.1)

Date: 2026-11-04 · Auditor: Claude.

## Why

The owner asked: "Everything is review by the skills?" Not as well as the audit files said.

- **What went wrong.** The 0.51.0–0.55.0 audits were written from memory of the checklists. Their
  line "applied from their `SKILL.md` and references" was not accurate: impeccable's
  `audit.native.md` and `android.md` had not been read in that session.
- **Gaps in the files.**
  - 0.51.0 had no ux-user-flows section; that skill landed in 0.51.1.
  - 0.55.0's graphic-design-fundamentals section was a paragraph.
  - No audit had less-is-more's own score table.

This pass loaded all four skills through the Skill tool: ux-user-flows, less-is-more,
graphic-design-fundamentals and impeccable. It read impeccable's `audit.native.md`, `android.md`
and `craft-floor.md`, then went back over every screen those releases changed, in code and in its
screenshots. impeccable's `context` step ran too; it found no PRODUCT.md, so it ran as a scoped
audit of existing code.

- **P3, Sleep pressure:** the level at midnight depends on the night's wakings. The line says it's
