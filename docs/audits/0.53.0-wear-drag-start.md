# Audit: the watch tile, and dragging the start (0.53.0)

Date: 2026-11-04 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13;
less-is-more). The skills were applied from their `SKILL.md` and references in `.claude/skills/`.

## Scope

The owner: "work from 1 to 7". Item 2:
- **Drag the start:** the Day timeline's start time takes the same hold-and-drag as the title.
- **The Wear OS tile:** a `wear/` module with one tile, and on the phone `WearBridge` /
  `WearListener`.

Screens looked at: `day_dragging_a_start_light`, `day_filter_all_light`, `day_light`,
`tile_awake`, `tile_asleep`, `tile_feeding`, `tile_no_phone` (Paparazzi, small round watch).

## What failed first, and the fix

| Found | Fix |
|---|---|
| The time stayed old while held; only the line below changed | The time itself shows the new start, on `selected` |
| The big button ran past the round screen's edge | 72% of the screen's width |

## less-is-more

| Element | 1 Helps now? | 2 Duplicate? | 3 Understood once? | 4 Weak? | 5 Rare? | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The tile's status line | Yes: asleep or awake, since when | No | Yes | — | — | Kept, one line |
| A running clock on the tile | Would help | The notification has it | — | Costs battery | — | **Not built**: "since 14:20", redrawn each minute |
| Diaper and other kinds on the tile | Rare from the wrist | The phone's Log button | — | — | Yes | **Left out**: sleep and breasts only |
| The drag hint's second half | Yes, until the first drag | No | Yes | — | — | Kept; it hides after the first drag, as before |
| A separate "edit start" control | No: the time is already there | — | — | — | — | **Not added**: the time is the handle |

Taps: fixing a start goes from 3+ (open, edit, Save) to one drag. The tile adds a 1-tap path from
the wrist. No common entry costs more.

## impeccable (native audit, android.md)

| Dimension | Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Accessibility | 3/4 | The start drag has TalkBack actions ("Began 5 minutes earlier / later"). Tile chips are ≥ 48dp, and every label is a word. The Last mark on the tile is a text dot, not an outline (protolayout's compact chip has no border). |
| Performance | 4/4 | The tile is redrawn on change and each minute; there's no ticking clock. |
| Theming | 3/4 | Colours from `CTokens.Dark`: mint with onMint, feed with page, surface with ink. The tile uses the watch's system font, not Libre Franklin: protolayout can't load a font file. |
| Responsive | 4/4 | Sizes from the device's screen width; checked on the small round preset. |
| Anti-patterns | 4/4 | One mint action per screen, on the watch too. |

Findings: none at P0 or P1.
- **P2:** the watch's font is the system's. This is recorded here; the design system's one-family
  rule can't reach a tile.
- **P2:** the Last mark is a "•". A border isn't available on a compact chip.
- **P3:** the tile's time doesn't tick.
