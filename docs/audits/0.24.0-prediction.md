# Audit: the last nap in the prediction (0.24.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable critique, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13)

## Scope

`Predictor` phase 2 (the last nap's length moves the next window), one new line on the
prediction card, and `PredictorYearTest`. No new components or tokens; no design-system patch.

Screens looked at: `today_after_a_short_nap_light` (new), `today_light` (unchanged: its log has
no nap effect, so no line).

## Found while building (fixed before release)

| # | Severity | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Critical | A late nap ending at or after the usual bedtime gave a bedtime 0–11 minutes after waking (6 of ~1,060 synthetic predictions) | Bedtime is at least half the usual last window after waking; a test reproduces it |
| 2 | Important | The fit used bedtime windows, which shrink when the last nap is long, so on a real-looking log the slope came out 0 and nothing showed | Fit only nap-to-nap windows, within each slot |
