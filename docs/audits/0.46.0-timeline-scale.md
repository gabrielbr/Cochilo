# Audit: the timeline shows how long things lasted (0.46.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13;
less-is-more)

## Scope

The owner said: "each block is not proportional to its amount of time … 2 hours of sleep is more
time than 10 minutes of breastfeeding."

What changed:
- **Scale:** `domain/TimelineScale` (tested in `TimelineScaleTest`) sets each bar's height:
  `space1` × 1.5 × √minutes, from `space2` to 192dp.
- **Timeline rows:** the bar in `TimelineRow` has its own height, no longer the row's, and a drag
  follows the scale.
- **Awake:** `TimelineAwake` draws an outlined bar on the same scale.

Screens looked at: `day_hour_by_hour_light`, `day_filter_all_light`, `day_dragging_a_sleep_light`,
`day_today_expected_light`.

## What failed first, and the fix

| Found | Fix |
|---|---|
| Rows were at least 48dp and bars filled them, so a 20-minute feed and a 1-hour nap looked the same | The bar's height is its own, from TimelineScale |
| 1:1 would make a night 600dp, longer than the screen | A square-root scale: 10 minutes is 19dp, 2 hours 66dp, 10 hours 147dp |
| Awake was a dot, so a 3-hour awake stretch looked like a 20-minute one | An outlined bar on the same scale; no fill, so sleep still leads |
