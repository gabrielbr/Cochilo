Date: 2026-11-04 · Auditor: Claude (ux-user-flows §13; impeccable, native;
graphic-design-fundamentals §13; less-is-more). The skills were applied from their `SKILL.md` and

> **Corrected in 0.55.1** ([0.55.1-reaudit.md](0.55.1-reaudit.md)): this audit was written from memory of the skills' checklists, not with them loaded; the line above overstated it. The re-audit loaded all four and fixed what it found.

## Scope

The owner: "work from 1 to 7". Items 3 and 4:
- **Keep in sync when closed:** a switch in Settings › Sync; a foreground service (github build)
  on the live notification's id. If there's no live stretch, its notification reads "In sync with
  {partner}".
- **Who logged it:** `events.loggedBy` (Room 5 → 6, backup format 5); "Your initial on entries" in
  Settings › Sync; "by A" on the line of an entry from the partner's phone.

Screens looked at: `sync_settings_light`, `initial_sheet_light`, `entries_logged_by_partner_light`.

## Decisions, and why

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| The service wears the live notification | A second ongoing notification for sync would be noise (less-is-more 2); the parent already keeps this one |
| Off by default | It costs battery; the parent decides |
| Started from the app, not at boot | `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` would be another permission; opening Cochilo once is enough |
| The initial is one letter, typed | A name would crowd the line; picking from a list needs more taps |
| Your own entries show nothing | Only the other parent's mark answers "who did this?" |
| `""` for a partner entry with no initial | This phone never claims an entry it didn't log |

## ux-user-flows

**Job stories.**
- *When we're both looking after the baby and I log something, I want it on the other phone
  without opening Cochilo there, so neither of us logs it twice.*
- *When an entry looks wrong, I want to know which of us logged it, so I ask the right person.*

**The flows** (in docs/flows.md, Partner sync):
1. Keep in sync when closed: Settings › Sync › the switch (on) → the service starts at once, on
   the live notification → changes go across with both apps closed → switch off, or Unpair →
   the service stops.
2. Your initial: Settings › Sync › Your initial on entries → a letter → Save (or Cancel) → the
   row shows it; it stays empty to show nothing.
3. Reading who: an entry from the other phone ends "by A"; no step.
