# Audit: From Tatá, a tab of its own (0.44.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13;
less-is-more)

## Scope

The owner said: "The card with Tatá's tips and content, I can't find. This should be a top level
menu item in the bottom nav bar." He decided:
- all twelve age notes are approved and signed;
- the tab is called "From Tatá / Da Tatá".

What changed:
- **New tab:** `ui/screens/TataTab.kt`, the fourth tab, with `gg_ic_heart` (design system 0.30.0).
- **Kit:** `TataNote` now has an optional byline and an optional dismiss.
- **Catalog:** `catalog.json` approves all the notes (`CatalogTest`).
- **Patterns:** no longer has a Tatá card.
- **Docs:** README, CHANGELOG, flows.md, tablet.md, in-app-marketing.md §13, roadmap.

Screens looked at:
- `tata_tab_light`, `tata_tab_this_week_light`, `tata_tab_ptBR_dark`;
- `today_font200_light`, `today_tablet_light`, `patterns_light`.

## What failed first, and the fix

| Found | Fix |
|---|---|
| The tab icon, `gg_ic_note`, is a pencil: on a tab of her notes it read as "edit" | `gg_ic_heart`, added to the design system first (0.30.0, PR #8) |
| Her byline under every note repeated the same line three or four times | Said once, under the title; the notes are unsigned in the tab |
| A "Got it" on each note would empty the tab | No close in the tab; the notes are the tab's content |
