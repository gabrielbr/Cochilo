## Scope

The owner: "work from 1 to 7". Item 1, the prediction's clock part:
- `domain/Clock.kt`, used by `Predictor`: the first nap and bedtime lean towards the baby's times.
- `domain/Pressure.kt` and the kit's `PressureLine`: today's sleep pressure on the Day tab.
- Today's "How it's guessed": one more line when the clock moved the guess.

Screens looked at: `day_pressure_light` (tall), `day_today_expected_light`, `today_light`.

## What failed first, and the fix

| Found | Fix |
|---|---|
| The clock made a baby whose naps only follow waking 4 minutes worse (synthetic) | It counts only as far as the time of day is steadier than the window: 11.9 vs 11.9 now |
| "100%%" showed on screen | The number goes through the format string |

## ux-user-flows

**Job story.** *When I'm planning the morning, I want the first-nap guess to know my baby
naps around 9:00, so I'm not caught out by a guess made from the wake time alone.*

The flow doesn't change: Today's card → (one tap) How it's guessed → the clock's line. The Day
tab → today → Sleep pressure, its "i" one tap away.
