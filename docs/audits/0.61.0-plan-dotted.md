# 0.61.0: the plan on the list, dotted, a clearer breath (four-skill audit)

The owner raised three things:
- The vertical timeline showed only the next projected sleep, not the day's pattern.
- He wanted dotted lines instead of dashed, everywhere, "so it's easier".
- He wasn't sure he saw the next nap breathing.

**How each skill was used.** All four were loaded with the Skill tool earlier in this session
(this conversation): `ux-user-flows`, `less-is-more`, `impeccable` (with `audit.native.md`,
`android.md`, `craft-floor.md` and `critique.md` read) and `graphic-design-fundamentals`. Their
checklists were applied to this change's screenshots. There's no emulator, so the breath was
checked as a still frame at its soft end (`ring_breath_soft_light`, `breathOverride`), not as
motion. The new and changed screenshots are `day_timeline_projection_light`,
`day_morning_projection_light`, `day_expected_*`, `day_today_expected_light` and
`day_pressure_light`.

## 1. ux-user-flows
**Job:** when I plan the day, I want every nap and bedtime with its time in the list I scroll, so
I don't have to read the ring. No tap was added.

**States:** morning (all the naps and bed), afternoon (one nap and bed), napping (bed only),
past window (said plainly), no plan.

§13 ✅: the list and the ring now agree (heuristic #4, consistency).

## 2. impeccable
**Critique:** the next sleep leads on both views (teal, breathing). Later ones are quieter but
readable. The halo makes the breath visible on a short nap without a flash.

**Finding (P2, fixed):** given more than one day of entries, the ring counted instant entries
(diapers) from other days as "until now" and stacked them ("+55"). An instant entry is now placed
at its own time.
