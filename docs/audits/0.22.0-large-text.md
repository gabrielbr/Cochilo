# Audit: large text (0.22.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable critique, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13)

## Scope

The 200% font scale pass (roadmap §4; audit v0.9.0 P3). New screenshots at fontScale 2.0: Today,
Today asleep, the open Log menu, Day, History, Patterns and Settings.

## What the first 200% shots showed (fixed before release)

| # | Severity | Where | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Critical | Log menu, Diaper | The choices row ran out of width: "Dirty" became a dot and couldn't be read | Choices are a flow row; they wrap above the item |
| 2 | Important | Header | Fixed 64dp height cut the status line in half ("Awake · 40m") | `heightIn(min)` on Header and BackHeader |
| 3 | Important | Earlier chips | "15" broke inside its chip; "min ago" clipped off the card | Flow row, chip words never wrap |
| 4 | Important | Tab bar | "Patterns" clipped to "Pattern" | Tab word capped at 150% (the icon says it too), min height |
| 5 | Polish | Patterns rhythm | "00" broke into two lines; "Yesterday" clipped | Axis ticks capped at 150%, the day column widens with them |
| 6 | Polish | Day ring | "06" touched the ring | Tick band widens with the capped scale |
| 7 | Polish | Day counts | "Diapers:" and its "4" split across lines | Non-breaking space in the counts |
