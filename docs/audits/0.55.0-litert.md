## ux-user-flows

**Job story.** *When Ask can't answer from its list, I want the AI that fits my phone, and to know
its size before I download it, so I don't fill the phone or wait on a slow one.*

The flow is the same: Settings › On-device AI (or Ask's "Get the on-device AI") → the sheet says
the size → Download → progress (one bar across both parts for Gemma 3n) → Ready.
