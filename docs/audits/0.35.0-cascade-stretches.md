# Audit: asleep and awake, and the Log menu's cascade (0.35.0)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable critique, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13)

## Scope

Two asks from the owner:
- the floating Log menu should open item by item, with a little movement and position, so it feels
  less rigid;
- the timelines should read as the two main states, sleeping and awake, with what happened during
  each set in under it.

What changed:
- `Motion.cascadeIn` and `LogButton` (the arc, `arcOffset`, tested in `ArcTest`).
- `domain/Stretches.kt` (`StretchesTest`).
- The Day screen's `VerticalTimeline`; `TimelineAwake`, `TimelineRow(indent)`, `ListRow(indent)`,
  `AwakeMark` and History's `StretchList`.
- The design system 0.26.0 adds `motion-stagger` 35ms and the components.md rows (patch 0023,
  drift 0).

Screens looked at:
- `day_hour_by_hour_light`, `day_dragging_a_sleep_light`, `day_today_expected_light`;
- `history_light`, `history_foldable_dark`, `history_font200_light`;
- `log_button_open_ptBR_dark`, `log_button_open_font200_light`, `log_button_feed_right_light`.

## What failed first, and the fix

| Found | Fix |
|---|---|
| The nested entries' times sat in the time column, so it read 7:50 PM, then 11:00 PM, then 10:30 PM, out of order. | The time column shows only the stretches' starts. A nested entry's time is in its meta. |
| Paparazzi caught the cascade at its first frame, with every row invisible. | `cascadeIn(still = preview != null)` for screenshots; the motion itself is unchanged. |

## Decisions

| Question | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Is awake a colour? | No: a hollow ring outlined in `muted` | Event colours are data; awake isn't an entry |
| Does awake get an icon? | No | Icons belong to kinds of entry; awake isn't one |
| Sleep or awake bigger? | Sleep stays a size up (label role, lead round) | Sleep leads |
| History filtered to one kind? | Stays a plain list | Grouping one kind says nothing |
| Can awake rows be tapped? | No, they're headings | Nothing to edit; no new tap |
| Cascade delay? | 35ms a row, 250ms each | The last of 8 rows lands in about half a second, under the 0.5s a menu should take |
| Closing? | All together | Leaving shouldn't wait |
