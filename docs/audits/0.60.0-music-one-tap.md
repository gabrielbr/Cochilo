# 0.60.0: the routine music, one tap away (four-skill audit)

The owner: "We need to have a quick access to the music." He asked for three things:
- Play music on the notification when sleep is near and while the baby sleeps.
- A secondary button under Start sleep on Today ("Start Music to sleep, or something friendly").
- An evaluation of intent with the user-flows skill, and other places to suggest.

**How each skill was used.** All four were loaded with the Skill tool earlier in this session
(this conversation): `ux-user-flows`, `less-is-more`, `impeccable` (with `audit.native.md`,
`android.md`, `craft-floor.md` and `critique.md` read) and `graphic-design-fundamentals`. Their
checklists were applied to this change. There is no emulator here:
- **Screens** were judged from Paparazzi (`today_music_light`,
  `today_night_waking_music_ptBR_light`).
- **The notification buttons** are standard actions. They weren't rendered, because Paparazzi
  can't draw the notification shade.

## 1. ux-user-flows

**Intent.** When she's about to put the baby down, or the baby stirs, she wants the lullabies on
at once, one-handed, often in the dark, so her hands stay with the baby. So the music belongs
where sleep starts or a resettle happens: Today's sleep card, the reminder before the window, and
the notification while asleep or in a waking. Not in Settings.

The flow, every entry and the taps are in [flows.md](../flows.md) ("Music one tap away"). The
other places considered (widget, watch tile, an opt-in "play when sleep starts") are there and in
the roadmap, for the owner to choose.

## 2. impeccable

Native Android, Operate mode.
- **Critique:** the button sits where the hand already is (under Start sleep), one weight below
  the mint action, so the hierarchy holds: Start sleep first, the music second.
- **Notifications:** the button is a third action only when sleep is near, so the shade stays
  quiet the rest of the day.
