# Audit: the rest of the day (0.49.0)

Date: 2026-11-04 · Auditor: Claude (impeccable, native; graphic-design-fundamentals §13;
less-is-more)

## Scope

The owner pointed at Napper's projected naps and asked how Cochilo could do the same. He then
said "go with your recommendation":
- the "Then:" line on Today, without a ring there;
- the age's nap count, capped by bedtime.

What changed:
- **Projection:** `domain/DayPlan.kt` (tested in `DayPlanTest`); `Predictor.predict(maxNaps)`.
- **Today:** the card's "Then:" line, and a line in "How it's guessed".
- **Day:** the ring's dashed projections and "Expected today: …", which TalkBack reads with the
  ring.

Screens looked at: `today_light`, `today_ptBR_light`, `today_dark`, `today_font200_light`,
`day_today_expected_light`.

## What failed first, and the fix

| Found | Fix |
|---|---|
| The age's nap count had no way to end the day: the predictor kept guessing naps past it | `predict(maxNaps)` turns the next sleep into bedtime once that many naps are done |
| Every projected nap with the same range claimed false precision | Each step widens the next one's range by half of its own, up to 45 minutes |
| A filled projected arc would read as a logged sleep | Dashed outlines, closed by end ticks, never filled |
| At night "Then: bed …" would be noise | No plan at night: asleep for the night, or awake in it |
