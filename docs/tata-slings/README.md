# Cochilo as a Tatá Slings product

Plans, drafted 2026-11-02, for making Cochilo a subproduct of [Tatá Slings](https://tataslings.com.br),
Thaíssa "Tatá" Vale's babywearing brand (since 2015, Ouro Branco, MG). Nothing here changes the app
yet; each document ends with the decisions Tatá needs to make.

| Document | What it answers |
|---|---|
| [monetization.md](monetization.md) | How Cochilo makes money without breaking "nothing leaves the phone": free app, Cochilo+ given with every sling through an offline-signed code, paid guides by Tatá, partner codes. Rough numbers and a phased plan. |
| [brand-voice.md](brand-voice.md) | Tatá's voice, tone and values, extracted from her site, with verbatim quotes and Cochilo strings rewritten in it. |
| [design-system.md](design-system.md) | The Tatá Slings design system: measured from the live site (teal `#5DC2BB`, navy `#13293D`, Libre Franklin), extended with a warm fabric palette, contrast-checked, mapped onto Cochilo's components. |
| [positioning-and-marketing.md](positioning-and-marketing.md) | Positioning ("Toda soneca conta. Até no colo."), audience, competition, launch phases, channels, content and SEO plan, targets, risks. |
| [preview.html](preview.html) | A visual preview of the palette, controls and two Cochilo screens in the new look (open in a browser; light and dark). |
| [../../design-system/tata-slings/tokens.json](../../design-system/tata-slings/tokens.json) | The proposed `tata` token layer, to go into the Gabriel Design System the same way the `cochilo` layer did. |

Sources: tataslings.com.br (home, "Tudo sobre a Tatá", "Tudo sobre o Tatá Fácil Sling", "Tudo
sobre o Tatá Swaddle", the Fácil Sling category, a product page, the FAQ and the Nuvemshop theme
CSS); the family's own notes on the store; Napper's Brazilian App Store prices; and
[docs/research.md](../research.md).
